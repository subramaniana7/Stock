NSE MONTHLY OHLC

Windows task: NSE Monthly OHLC
Schedule: the 1st of every month at 09:00 India Standard Time.
First future scheduled run: 1 December 2026, producing November 2026.
Initial backfill: October 2026, produced immediately during setup.

Each file is separate: outputs\YYYY-MM\Monthly Mon YYYY.xlsx
Example: outputs\2026-10\Monthly Oct 2026.xlsx
Sheets: Monthly OHLC, Daily Prices, Sources and Method, CPR Guide.
Columns match weekly reports, including the weekly six CPR bands,
three-decimal classification, colours and comma-formatted TradingView symbols.

Monthly Open: first selected trading session's open.
Monthly High: maximum daily high across the selected sessions.
Monthly Low: minimum daily low across the selected sessions.
Monthly Close: final selected trading session's close.
Universe: union of stock underlyings with STF/STO contracts during the month.
Prices: cash EQ/F1, INR per share, unadjusted. Index contracts are excluded.
Every stock requires valid cash prices across all selected sessions.
Official ISIN matches consolidate symbol renames into the latest cash symbol.
The supporting Daily Prices sheet records each original Source Cash Symbol;
Sources and Method documents any rename mapping.
Missing or invalid data prevents partial workbook export and remains pending.

The monthly task uses separate cache, lock, completion receipts and logs.
It does not change daily/weekly schedules or their data files.
Catch-up: run after a missed start and one minute after Windows sign-in.
Retries: three attempts, 15 minutes apart. Pending months retry on later runs.
PC must be on and the configured Windows user signed in. The task requests
wake-from-sleep, subject to Windows/hardware support. Codex/Excel need not be open.

Email: the existing sender sends a plain-text notification after successful
monthly creation; no attachment. Email failures are queued for later retry.

Configuration: monthly_config.json. first_month controls earliest backfill.
additional_sessions lists confirmed special trading dates; additional_closures
lists confirmed closures beyond the official annual calendar. The known
8 November 2026 Muhurat session is included. Future special sessions must be
added when NSE announces them. Weekday holidays use the official CM/FO calendar.

Status: monthly_last_run_status.json
Receipts: monthly_state.json
Cache and source payloads: monthly_data
Log: logs\monthly_update.log
Task backup: Monthly Task Scheduler.xml

Manual generation: python monthly_update.py --month 2026-10
Offline regeneration: add --offline after all source files are cached.
Source download/validation without workbook/email: add --fetch-only.
To disable only monthly updates, disable NSE Monthly OHLC in Task Scheduler.
