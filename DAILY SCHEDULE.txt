NSE DAILY REPORTS

Windows task: NSE Daily OHLC (separate from NSE Weekly OHLC).
Checks each day at 16:30 IST and every 15 minutes through 22:00.
Additional catch-up checks: daily at 09:00, and one minute after sign-in.
The task requires the PC to be on and your Windows account signed in.
It requests wake-from-sleep, subject to Windows/hardware power support.
Three restart attempts at 15-minute intervals follow a failed run.

The task checks the actual availability of BOTH official final NSE files,
cash (CM) and derivatives (FO), before adding a date. Publication time is not
assumed. Closures come from the NSE CM/FO holiday calendar. Weekend sessions
can be entered in daily_config.json: additional_sessions; any closure override
goes in additional_closures. No blank holiday or ordinary weekend sheets.

One new workbook per Monday-Friday week in the SAME local outputs folder:
NSE_FO_Daily_OHLC_YYYY-MM-DD_to_YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx
One dated sheet per trading day, plus Sources containing URL/hash metadata.
Daily columns: Stock Symbol, Daily Open, Daily High, Daily Low, Daily Close.
Prices are INR cash EQ/F1, unadjusted. Each day's stock universe comes from
that day's STF/STO derivatives records. Index contracts are excluded.

Initial backfill starts on Monday 9 November 2026. All pending dates since
then are checked on subsequent runs, including after shutdown or an outage.
Successful dates are not duplicated. A new week creates a new workbook.
Keep the workbook closed during scheduled updates; if Excel locks the file,
the existing copy is preserved and the update stays pending for retry.

The daily program regenerates its managed weekly workbook from its validated
daily_data/weeks JSON records when adding a day. Do not add personal notes
or manual edits to these generated workbooks: keep an analysis copy instead.

Daily scripts/config/cache are independent. The Saturday task definition,
weekly program/configuration and weekly report files are unchanged.

Status: daily_last_run_status.json
Log: logs\daily_update.log
Daily prices/calendar/archive cache: daily_data
Daily workbook builder: daily_runtime\build_daily.mjs
Task backup: Daily Task Scheduler.xml

To run manually, select NSE Daily OHLC in Windows Task Scheduler and Run.
To stop daily updates, Disable NSE Daily OHLC. The Saturday task continues.
Keep this folder and the installed Codex bundled Python/Node runtime available;
Codex and Excel do not have to be open while the task runs.
